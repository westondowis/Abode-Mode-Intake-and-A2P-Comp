Privacy Policy & SMS Safeguards

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Operating Entity: Abode Mode LLC (abodebuys.com / abode-mode.com)

No mobile information will be shared with third parties/affiliates for marketing/promotional purposes. All other categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

1. Information We Collect

Abode Mode collects information directly from property owners and authorized representatives when submitting parcel details, requesting a written cash offer, or opting in to receive SMS transaction updates. This includes:

  • Contact Information: Full legal name, mobile telephone number, email address, and mailing address.
  • Property & Parcel Details: Physical street address, county, state, Assessor Parcel Number (APN), acreage, zoning, utility connections, and ownership standing.
  • Telephony & Consent Records: Date and timestamp of opt-in, originating IP address, web user-agent string, exact disclosure version affirmed, and communication transcripts.

2. Purpose of Information Use

We use the collected information strictly for legitimate commercial real estate acquisition activities, including:

  • Evaluating parcel title, easements, market valuation, and preparing formal written purchase contracts.
  • Sending transaction-related SMS notifications, offer status updates, inspection schedule reminders, and closing milestone alerts.
  • Complying with statutory real estate transaction recording and telecommunications carrier compliance protocols.

3. Text Messaging (SMS) Originator Opt-In Data Protection

We strictly enforce a non-sharing covenant across all messaging programs. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are strictly excluded from all data sharing, sales, rentals, or promotional transfers.

Your mobile phone number and opt-in consent record will never be shared with, sold to, or leased to any external third party, data aggregator, marketing broker, or affiliate under any circumstances.

4. Carrier Opt-Out & Support Protocols

You have full autonomy over your mobile communications at all times:

  • Opt-Out: Reply STOP to any SMS message from Abode Mode to instantly cancel future text messages. You will receive one final confirmation text acknowledging your cancellation.
  • Customer Support: Reply HELP to any message, call our acquisitions desk directly at (720) 649-0734, or email wdowis@abode-mode.com.
  • Frequency & Rates: Message frequency varies based on transaction milestones. Standard message and data rates may apply depending on your mobile carrier plan.
  • Independence: Consent to receive SMS messages is completely voluntary and is not a condition of purchasing, selling, or contracting with Abode Mode LLC.

5. Data Retention & Security Safeguards

We implement industry-standard encryption, strict database access controls, and audited cloud storage protocols to protect your personal and property records against unauthorized access, loss, or disclosure.
